Much later than planned (check [my post](/blog/lets-start-preperations-to-scwcd) when I revealed my intent to pass
SCWCD) I managed to get this certificate. The result (94%) is in my opinion more than good 🙂 Preparations began
a fairly long time ago (over a year ago) but since this time there were some unexpected circumstances (mainly a project
which seemed to grow and grow) and that's why SCWCD mission took me so long.

I started preparations as usual, by downloading specifications and adding API to Firefox bookmarks (list of links
[here](/blog/lets-start-preperations-to-scwcd)). Then I bought
Head First Servlets and JSP, the most recommended book for this
certificate. I had ambitiously assumed that I would be able to write more detailed posts about what I learnt but
unfortunately lack of time didn't allow me to do it.

I read the book twice in a fairly considerable period of time. The first reading was to revise servlets and read about
areas I hadn't met before (mainly Custom Tags). This time I didn't concentrate on details, it was the purpose of the
second reading. Simultaneously with reading I started experimenting with NetBeans and writing simple JSPs, creating
web.xml manually, etc.

The final impulse to take the exam was a promotion from Sun connected with Kirk Pepperdine's talk at Warsaw JUG (more
about this promotion in Polish can be found at
[Jacek Laskowski's blog](http://jlaskowski.blogspot.com/2010/01/pakiety-certyfikacyjne-od-suna-za-600.html)). I saved
about 300 Polish Zlotys, bought an exam voucher and an e-simulator with example questions which at the end turned out
to be very similar to the real exam (not surprising at all 🙂). This simulator, free mocks and notes available at
[JavaRanch Forum](https://web.archive.org/web/20100211142449/http://faq.javaranch.com/java/ScwcdLinks) and questions from Head First book were more than enough
and this time I didn't buy a simulator from WhizLabs or Enthuware.

And what do I think about SCWCD itself? Unfortunately I didn't like it as much as SCJP. That time I felt that
everything I was learning would be useful, and now at SCWCD learning path, I had to memorize things I DEFINITELY won't
use in real projects (custom tags especially). Although some chapters were interesting and I think every developer
should know how to manually build a web application, how a servlet works and what design patterns could be useful for
various scenarios. Obviously knowledge about JSP tags is also desirable, especially when you are joining an older
project written in this technology.

To sum up, SCWCD certificate is good to have but some stuff you have to familiarize yourself with could make you
sad/sick/depressed/angry (choose at least one).
