2016 was the first full year when the [JVM Bloggers](https://jvm-bloggers.com/) project was running so to sum up this period of time I have gathered some numbers to share with you.

### 19

Number of newsletter issues published on our website. Before the application was launched, issues were sent only to Polish JUGs’ mailing lists.

### 49

Number of new blogs added to the JVM Bloggers project so they could be monitored. 33 of them were personal blogs, 8 were from IT companies and 8 from video channels. To add your blog create a Pull Request with a new entry to one of [these files](https://jvm-bloggers.com/issue/50).

### 50

Issue #50 was the first published online at [our website](https://jvm-bloggers.com/issue/50) at 28th August.

### 118

Number of blog posts published by the most active blogger – [Koziołek](http://koziolekweb.pl/).

### 526

Number of commits in 2016 in the master branch. Below are more detailed stats:

### 1306

During 2016 we have published 1306 articles (blog posts, video recordings, etc.). This gives 25 articles to read every week published at JVM Bloggers.

### 2883

Number of messages posted on our Slack channel where we discuss new features and occasional bugs.
