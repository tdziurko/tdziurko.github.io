It's been some time since I passed my Sun Certified Java Programmer (SCJP) version 1.5, but maybe this post will help
somebody to choose his way to prepare for this exam and also increase his chances to score better.

## Useful stuff

- [SCJP Sun Certified Programmer for Java 5 Study Guide (Exam 310-055)](http://www.amazon.com/Certified-Programmer-310-055-Certification-Guides/dp/0072253606),
  simply "must have" for anyone planning to get his own, shiny SCJP certificate. This book covers everything you may
  encounter at the exam. I read it once from cover to cover and repeated chapters that contain areas I didn't feel
  comfortable with (Generics, Threads).
- exam simulator [EnthuWare JQ+](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604064031/http://www.enthuware.com/jqplusv5/), it costs only 25$ or 18$ (discount for students)
  and it helped me significantly to achieve a better result
- your favorite IDE to test your ideas, doubts and just to play with example questions from the book and simulator.
- [Mariusz Lipiński's blog](http://www.mariuszlipinski.pl/search/label/SCJP) (beware: it's in Polish) where the author
  describes his preparations to the exam in a form similar to "Two minute drill" from Sierra & Bates book
- [JavaRanch SCJP Forum](http://www.coderanch.com/forums/f-24/Programmer-Certification-SCJP) where you can meet
  the authors of the book I mentioned above

## General tips

- If you don't have experience with using Java language in various projects better do all tests you can get (book,
  simulator, free tests from the Internet) because after doing it your efficiency in finding traps and nuances will
  increase a lot.
- Money spent on an exam simulator is never wasted money
- Experiment, play any time when you are unsure whether something works this way or another. Are you wondering what
  would happen if this question looked a little different? Don't only think, launch your IDE and check any variation
  that comes to your mind. My experiments with Netbeans gave me a lot of knowledge and confidence that I really
  understand the topic.
- Don't extend your preparations for too long. Pick a date, even in 3-4 months, when you are going to take the exam
  and consequently try to make it. Having such a "deadline" in your head you will be more determined and regular.
  I have a friend who has been studying for SCJP for more than half a year and his preparations seem to be endless 🙂
